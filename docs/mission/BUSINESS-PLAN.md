# NOVERA business plan — founder review draft

## 1. Executive Summary

NOVERA is an independent concept-stage venture. First product hypothesis: NOVERA ONE wheeled indoor visitor-guidance research platform. This plan is a founder review draft dated 13 November 2026. Incorporation, revenue, funding, staff and customer traction are unverified. Near-term objective: create reproducible evidence and validate a narrow customer problem before capital commitments.

## 2. Vision and Mission

Develop human-centered robotics grounded in measurable research, accessible interaction and supervised operation.

## 3. Problem Definition

Facilities managers, museums, university visitor centres and controlled indoor reception teams may face costly uncertainty in visitor guidance, repeated inquiries and indoor navigation. Interview whether existing staff, signs or kiosks already suffice.

## 4. Proposed Solution

NOVERA ONE wheeled indoor visitor-guidance research platform. Software prototypes demonstrate workflow only. No physical-product, regulated engineering or operational permission claim.

## 5. Product Portfolio

ONE is primary; preserve ten general and fifteen specialized concepts as exploratory visual concepts. No medical, childcare, security or emergency-response competence.

## 6. Market Research

Use the dated source register below. IFR service-robot categories indicate an industry landscape, but do not establish an addressable visitor-guidance market. TAM/SAM/SOM: TBD. Obtain segment counts, procurement budgets, interview records and bottom-up reachable account estimates before quoting market size.

## 7. Customer Segments

Facilities managers, museums, university visitor centres and controlled indoor reception teams. Buyer, budget owner, operator and affected user may differ. Accessibility and privacy stakeholders must participate. No customer segment is validated.

## 8. Competitor Analysis

PAL Robotics ARI and Pudu are primary-source landscape examples. Compare task scope, supervision, navigation interfaces, total service burden, privacy and verified operating evidence. Their existence establishes alternative solution categories, not NOVERA differentiation or demand. Human reception, accessible signage and kiosks may solve the problem at lower integration burden.

## 9. Business Model

Compare supervised pilot support, research software integration and maintenance scopes before considering hardware sale or Robot-as-a-Service. RaaS could bundle hardware, monitoring and service; it creates fleet financing, repair and liability burdens. Hardware sales require sourced BOM, assembly yield, warranty reserves and compliance. Neither model is price-validated. No pilot fee or customer revenue has been earned in the evidence inspected.

## 10. Go-to-Market Strategy

First 30 days: create consent-based interview list; define five problem questions and evidence criteria. Days 31–60: seek founder-authorized interviews and record current workaround, procurement requirements, objections and willingness-to-pay. Days 61–90: decide whether to stop, narrow scope or pursue a legal, supervised pilot. Interview targets are targets, never reported as completed. No outreach was sent by this work.

## 11. Technical Development Roadmap

Gate 1: correct Candidate B geometry and mass/power arithmetic; Gate 2: ROS/Gazebo spawn, sensors and bridges; Gate 3: SLAM, saved-map localization, Nav2 mission and fault tests; Gate 4: independent physical safety design; Gate 5: supervised bench/prototype testing. Local 2D simulation is distinct from Gate 2–3. No purchased hardware or actual robot performance.

## 12. Operations

Founder is decision owner, not proof of a staffed company. Assign software reviewer, engineering safety reviewer, privacy reviewer and finance/accounting reviewer before release. Maintain source/claim register and reviewed pull requests. No public client information in repository. Local tools are internal prototypes; external hosting/auth/storage require a separate privacy and security design.

## 13. Funding Requirements

Use financial-model.xlsx to estimate a gate-bounded cash requirement. No actual investment ask or valuation is justified yet. Obtain written engineering, hardware, legal and insurance quotes; confirm hiring capacity and taxes. EIC Accelerator is a public research lead only; eligibility, live call deadlines, legal status and co-financing remain unverified.

## 14. Financial Model

Editable workbook contains 36-month low/base/high assumptions, monthly costs, hypothetical receipts, gross margin, cash, runway flag, break-even and funding gap. Every entered number is an unquoted planning assumption, not a verified expense or forecast. Base receipts are zero. Hardware feasibility and specialist review are scoped allowances, not a complete vehicle/robot development budget. VAT, financing, depreciation and corporate tax are excluded pending accountant input.

## 15. Risks

Principal risks: no demonstrated buyer need, insufficient qualified delivery capacity, inaccurate technical assumptions, privacy and image rights, unclear IP ownership, legal trading ability and capital shortfall. Mobile collision, tipping, battery and misleading interaction require physical safety review.

## 16. Milestones

M1 (0–30 days target): reproduce repository tests and review scope. M2 (31–60): complete missing engineering integrations or bounded LABS delivery rehearsal. M3 (61–90): independently review evidence, costs and discovery findings. M4: founder decides continuation using measured gate evidence. These windows are planning targets, not delivery promises. See READINESS.md for actual status.
