01 / VISION

Make technology feel easier to talk to.

We want to explore humanoid robots that can help, explain, remember what you choose to share, and respond thoughtfully. Not machines pretending to be people. Tools built to work well with them.

02 / NOVERA ONE

A face. A voice. A reason to be useful.

NOVERA ONE is our proposed humanoid companion and assistant. The first milestone is a working digital personality. Next: expressive eyes, an articulated head and controlled arm gestures. Walking comes later, if testing and resources justify it.

03 / TECHNOLOGY

Thoughtful by design.

Emotion recognition is an uncertain inference from behavior, voice and language — not mind reading. NOVERA will not claim to experience feelings or consciousness.

04 / RESPONSIBILITY

Trust comes before personality.

The system should let people see when its microphone or camera is active, decide what can be remembered, delete stored information and switch off sensors. Physical movement must stay slow, limited and guarded during prototyping. It must not pretend to be a therapist, human friend or independent decision-maker.

05 / DEVELOPMENT

Start small. Test everything.

These are proposed milestones, not completed achievements or promised launch dates.

06 / FOUNDER

I want to build something people can actually connect with.

I've always been fascinated by the idea of a robot that could look at you, listen and respond in a way that feels natural. I know we're nowhere near that vision yet. I'm starting with the basics: learning, testing ideas and finding people who can help turn a concept into working technology.
