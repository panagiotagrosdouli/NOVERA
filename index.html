Independent concept-stage research. NOVERA ONE is not a built, tested, certified or commercially available robot. No orders or delivery commitments.

01 / Vision

Robotics that starts with people.

We believe the next chapter of robotics should be shaped by usability, trust and genuine real-world value. NOVERA is exploring how embodied AI systems could one day support people in everyday environments.

For now, this is a research and design direction — not a claim of working hardware or proven artificial intelligence capabilities.

02 / Principles

Thoughtful by design.

Our early questions focus on what matters most before any commercial promises: usefulness, safety and responsible interaction.

01

Human-centered

Begin with user needs and accessible experiences, not novelty alone.

02

Technically grounded

Evaluate engineering feasibility and AI limitations before claiming performance.

03

Responsible by default

Consider safety, privacy and user expectations from the earliest stages.
