ONE.

A humanoid built around a simple idea: technology should be easier to talk to.

Explore the concept

NOVERA ONE is an early-stage concept. This is reference photography, not our hardware.

01 / WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

A presence you can interact with.

NOVERA ONE starts with a conversational AI system and a face capable of expressive responses. We plan to add carefully controlled movement only when the basics are reliable. Our goal is usefulness first, humanoid complexity second.

03 / HUMAN INTERACTION

Expressions with a purpose.

Digital eyes, speech and subtle gestures can make it clearer when the robot is listening, thinking, uncertain or asking for help. These are designed expressions, not evidence of inner feelings.
