NOVERA ONE. A focused beginning.

Candidate B remains a differential-drive indoor study: 500 × 500 × 1050 mm assumed envelope, 36 kg hypothetical allocation. Local 2D telemetry works separately from the unexecuted ROS 2 Jazzy / Gazebo Harmonic integration scaffold.

Η μελέτη Candidate B παραμένει τροχήλατη για εσωτερικούς χώρους. Διαστάσεις και μάζα είναι υποθετικές. Η τοπική δισδιάστατη προσομοίωση διακρίνεται από τη μη εκτελεσμένη ενσωμάτωση ROS/Gazebo.

01 / MISSION

Indoor visitor guidance

Approved fixture destinations, bilingual scripted information and operator handoff. No medical, childcare or security role.

02 / IMPLEMENTATION

A visible software state

Local 2D simulation, modeled LiDAR, mission supervision and fault handling. Full ROS/Gazebo integration remains unverified.

03 / NEXT GATE

Validate before hardware

Reproduce sensor and navigation tests, source components and independently review physical safety.
