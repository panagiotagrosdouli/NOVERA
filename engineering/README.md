# NOVERA ONE V1 — Engineering workspace

**Status:** concept / desk research. No functioning, assembled, tested, or certified robot exists. This workspace is independent of VELTARO.

## Baseline concept

A **wheeled indoor social-assistance robot**, not a walking humanoid. This is a pragmatic first engineering baseline, subject to feasibility testing. Its roles are multilingual guidance, scripted conversation, and supervised visitor assistance. It is **not** a medical, childcare, safety-critical or autonomous security service.

## Design discipline

Treat every numerical target as **provisional** until substantiated by tests, drawings, simulations or datasheets. Do not claim autonomy, voice AI integration, navigation performance, regulatory compliance or product availability based on website demos.

## Next design gate

Approve the use case and envelope requirements; choose a drive layout; create a dimensioned CAD envelope and first mass/power budget; then evaluate components and prototype costs. **Spending and contacting suppliers require explicit approval.**
