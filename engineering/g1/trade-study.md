# Concept alternatives and provisional decision

The following is a **qualitative design discussion**, not scored evidence.

| Decision factor | A compact | B balanced | C tall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor clearance footprint | Best nominal | Moderate | Largest |
| Visitor display height | Lower | Middle | Higher |
| Top-heavy risk | Potentially less | Intermediate | Requires extra attention |
| Packaging space | Constrained | Moderate | More space, may add mass |
| Accessibility confidence | Needs testing | Needs testing | Needs testing |
| Build complexity | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown |

**G1 study baseline: candidate B**, because the 500 × 500 mm base and 1050 mm overall height offer an intermediate package for subsequent CAD trade-offs. This is a *study selection*, not a committed product configuration.
