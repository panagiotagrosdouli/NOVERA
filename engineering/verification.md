# Verification and risk plan

**Start with low-risk simulations and tabletop rigs.** Do not put an unverified mobile robot among the public.

Initial risk register:
1. Collision with people or objects: limit speed, supervised tests, physical E-stop, independent fault handling.
2. Pinch points and tipping: guarded moving parts, center-of-mass modeling, stability testing.
3. Battery thermal/electrical incidents: appropriate certified modules, protected wiring, fusing and qualified assembly.
4. Privacy harms: indicators, opt-in capture, minimal retention, clear data ownership.
5. Hallucinated guidance: scoped tasks, reviewed scripts/maps, graceful handoff to human staff.
6. Accessibility exclusion: multiple modalities and user testing.

Verification stages:
- V0: requirements and hazard analysis reviewed.
- V1: CAD envelope, mass/center-of-mass and energy budget checked.
- V2: drivetrain bench test with safety controls and fault injection.
- V3: low-speed indoor navigation with no bystanders.
- V4: supervised structured user tests in controlled space.
- V5: compliance review **before** any deployment or sales claim.

Potentially relevant safety and regulatory standards must be evaluated against the final intended use and market by qualified professionals. No compliance certification is claimed.
